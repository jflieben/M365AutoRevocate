Safety limits (circuit breaker)

If a run would act on more users than these limits, the tool pauses all processing and asks you to review before anything else happens. This is your protection against a bulk mistake (a mass disable, an accidental sync deletion, a first inactivity scan in an old tenant).

Always ignore (global exclusions)

These accounts are never touched by any action, at any trigger (inactive, disable or delete). Use this for break-glass and service accounts, and to keep resource mailboxes out of offboarding entirely.

Exclusion group
Members are never acted on, at any trigger. Pick from the suggestions.
